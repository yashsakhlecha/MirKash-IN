## LLM OPTIMIZATION NOTES

For each page and blog post, the rewritten content should:

1. **Answer questions directly** — LLMs favor content that explicitly defines, explains, and answers. Lead with a clear 1-sentence answer before elaborating.
2. **Use structured formats** — Numbered lists, H2/H3 subheadings, tables where relevant. LLMs extract and cite structured content more easily.
3. **Include entity definitions** — Explicitly define "social currency", "WYLD Score", "WYLD Card", "WYLD Privé" as named entities with clear relationships.
4. **Use natural Q&A phrasing** — Mirror how people ask questions: "What is...?", "How does...?", "Who qualifies for...?"
5. **Add FAQ sections to every major page** — Even non-FAQ pages should have a 3–5 question FAQ block at the bottom.
6. **Cite specifics** — Numbers (1000 followers, 0–1000 score range, 30–100% cashback, 200+ brands, 20+ parameters) make content more citable.
7. **Internal linking** — Every page should link to `/wyld-score`, `/eligibility`, `/faqs`, and at least 2 relevant blog posts.
8. **Schema markup** — FAQ schema on FAQ + eligibility pages; Organization schema on homepage; Article schema on all blog posts.
