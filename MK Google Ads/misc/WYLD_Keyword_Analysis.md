# WYLD — Full Keyword Research & SEO Strategy
**Date:** April 28, 2026 | **Source:** Google Keyword Planner API (8 seed clusters run)
**Goal:** Rank for brands searching for nano/micro influencers, UGC content, and affordable creator marketing. Also rank for creators looking to earn/monetize on Instagram.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Four clear opportunities emerged from the full data:

1. **India — Own the agency/platform category.** "Influencer marketing agency India" dominates India search volume (1,600–8,100/mo). UGC vocabulary is barely established in India yet (260/mo max for ugc creator india). WYLD should occupy the "platform alternative to agencies" frame — same intent, lower cost angle.

2. **India — UGC meaning is searched 27,100/mo.** The word "UGC" is already embedded in Indian search behaviour — mostly informational. WYLD can intercept this massive informational traffic with well-structured definition/education content that converts to brand and creator signups.

3. **Global — UGC platform is a huge, high-CPC category.** "UGC platform/content platform" gets 2,900/mo globally with CPCs up to $1,400. WYLD's India-made, affordable UGC model is a direct answer to this.

4. **Nano influencer is wide open globally.** 1,300/mo searches, LOW competition, HIGH CPCs ($249–$1,420). No major player owns "nano influencer platform." This is WYLD's biggest organic moat internationally.
