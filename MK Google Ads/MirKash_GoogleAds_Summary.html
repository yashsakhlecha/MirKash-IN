Mir Kash — Google Ads Setup

Complete account build summary  ·  April 2026  ·  Prepared by Claude

1. Overview

Mir Kash is a premium vegan leather handbag brand based in India, selling through two Shopify storefronts — mirkash.in (INR) and mirkash.com (USD). Hero products are the Braidey and Weaver Tote. All other SKUs are catalogue products.

The goal of this Google Ads build was to capture three distinct buyer intents — conscious/vegan buyers, working women shopping for a laptop bag, and gift buyers — while also running a Shopping campaign to intercept broad handbag category searches.

3. Campaign Strategy & Rationale

Campaign 1 — Vegan Believers (₹30K/mo, largest budget)

This is Mir Kash's most differentiated angle. No major Indian brand is directly targeting the "vegan leather" search intent, which means CPCs are lower and competition is thin. Buyers searching for "vegan leather bag" or "apple leather bag" already believe in the product category — they just need to find a brand. This is the highest-intent, lowest-friction audience.

Two ad groups:

  • Vegan Leather Bags — core category terms searched by the aware buyer
  • Alternative Materials — apple leather, cactus leather, plant-based, cruelty-free terms for buyers at the research stage

Why biggest budget: This audience converts at higher rates because they're already sold on the material philosophy. Mir Kash is built for exactly this buyer.

Campaign 2 — Work / Weaver Tote (₹22.5K/mo)

The Weaver Tote has a clear functional use case — it fits a 13" laptop and works as an office bag. "Laptop bag for women India" and "office bag for women India" are high-volume, high-purchase-intent searches. This campaign captures buyers who aren't necessarily searching for vegan leather but are looking for a premium work bag.

Two ad groups:

  • Laptop Bags Women — laptop-specific terms, ads point directly to the Weaver Tote product page
  • Office Bags Women — broader office/work terms with the same destination

Why second-largest budget: Work bag searches have strong purchase intent and year-round demand. This campaign sells the Weaver Tote specifically, not just the brand.

Campaign 3 — Gifting (₹7.5K/mo)

Gifting searches spike around Diwali, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, and birthdays. A premium vegan leather bag is a natural answer to "luxury gift for wife India." The budget is intentionally lower because gifting is seasonal — in April (off-peak), volume will be low and CPCs near zero for Diwali terms. As festivals approach, this campaign will become more active and budget should be revisited.

Two ad groups:

  • Gifting Occasions — year-round terms: birthday, anniversary, Valentine's
  • Festive Gifting — seasonal terms: Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Women's Day

Campaign 4 — Category Discovery Shopping (₹12.5K/mo)

Shopping ads show product images, prices, and brand names directly in search results — ideal for a visually premium product like Mir Kash bags. Buyers searching "handbag for women India" or "crossbody bag women India" will see actual product photos, which builds brand awareness and drives discovery beyond just keyword-matched text ads.

Connected to Merchant Center ID 5768082829 (mirkash.in feed). Geo-targeted to India only.

Campaign 5 — International Shopping (₹27.5K/mo) — Pending

Will target US and UK via the mirkash.com store once its Google & YouTube Shopify app is connected. International buyers pay in USD/GBP and CPCs are higher, but so are average order values. Held until Merchant Center for .com is confirmed.

5. Technical Setup Completed

Conversion Tracking

Handled via the Google & YouTube Shopify app installed on mirkash.in and connected to mirkashco.in@gmail.com. No custom code was needed. Purchase conversions will appear under Tools → Conversions after the first sale is recorded.

Merchant Center Feed — Custom Labels

Custom labels were bulk-set via Shopify API across all 54 product variants on both stores:

  • hero — Braidey and Weaver Tote (all colorways)
  • standard — all other products

This allows future Shopping campaigns to be split by product tier and bid differently on hero vs. standard SKUs.

Google Feed Attributes Fixed

Merchant Center was flagging missing required attributes. Fixed via Shopify API across both stores (162 metafields set, 0 errors):

  • age_group → adult
  • gender → female
  • size → One Size

Ad Format

All Search campaigns use Responsive Search Ads (RSAs) — each ad group has 15 headlines and 4 descriptions. Google's machine learning tests combinations and serves the highest-performing permutation for each search query and user. This is the current best practice for Search ads.

6. Why This Setup Will Work

The short answer: Mir Kash has a genuinely differentiated product in a low-competition keyword space. The campaigns are structured to find buyers who already want what Mir Kash sells, not convince unaware buyers.

Low Competition on Core Keywords

"Vegan leather bag india" and related terms have meaningful search volume but very few direct competitors bidding on them in India. Most bag brands target generic "handbag women india" terms where they compete against Hidesign, Lavie, and budget brands on price. Mir Kash sidesteps that race entirely by owning the vegan/sustainable intent.

Intent-Matched Campaigns

Each campaign targets a specific moment in the buyer journey — not just a demographic. Someone searching "laptop bag for women india" is ready to buy a work bag today. Someone searching "diwali gift for wife" has a deadline and a budget. These are high-conversion moments, not awareness moments.

Product-Ad Alignment

Ads for the Work campaign link directly to the Weaver Tote product page, not the homepage. This reduces friction — the buyer lands exactly where they need to be. RSA copy was written specifically around the functional benefits (fits 13" laptop, ships free, desk to dinner) rather than generic luxury claims.

Shopping Ads for Visual Discovery

Vegan leather bags photograph well, and Mir Kash products are premium-looking. Shopping ads show the actual product image and price in search results — this builds brand recognition even when buyers don't click, and converts strongly when they do.

Correct Bidding Phase

Starting on Maximize Clicks (not Maximize Conversions) is the right call with a new account. Google needs 30+ conversions per month before its conversion bidding algorithm has enough data to optimize. Starting on clicks builds traffic and feeds the algorithm data, so the switch to conversion bidding later will be effective rather than blind.

8. What to Watch in the First 30 Days

  • Impressions but no clicks — usually means bids are too low or Quality Score is building. Wait 48–72 hours before adjusting.
  • Clicks but no conversions — check that the purchase conversion is firing (visit the site, buy a test order if needed). Also check landing page load speed.
  • High spend on broad irrelevant searches — review Search Terms report weekly for the first month and add negatives as needed.
  • Shopping campaign underperforming — usually a feed quality issue. Check Merchant Center diagnostics for disapprovals.
  • Campaign 2 (Work) outperforming — this is expected. "Laptop bag for women india" is a very concrete purchase query. If it's working well, consider increasing its budget at Week 3.
